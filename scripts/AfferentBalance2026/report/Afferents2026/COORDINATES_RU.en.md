# Coordinates and tensile torque

The plotted angle is θ = q + π/2; θ = 0 is the upper vertical. Positive θ points right in the schematic. Flexor and extensor lengths are √(a₁²+a₂² ∓ 2a₁a₂ sin θ). Moment arms are r_F = a₁a₂ cos θ / L_F and r_E = a₁a₂ cos θ / L_E. Their length velocities are −r_F ω and +r_E ω. Positive muscle tensions give M = r_F F_F − r_E F_E = −F_F ∂L_F/∂θ − F_E ∂L_E/∂θ. There is no factor 10. Gravity is destabilizing: J dω/dt = K_g sin θ − bω + M.

All manuscript figures use degrees for θ and radians/second for ω, except where a separate unit is explicitly stated. Training uses a 0.1 ms step; saving or plotting fewer samples does not change that step. Prior convention notes are preserved only in the archive.
