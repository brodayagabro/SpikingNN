Their relevance and the limits of comparisons with this model are documented in [the extended review](../Relate_works.md#literature-extension-20261030).

The selection criterion is the journal's best **SCImago SJR 2025** quartile, matching the original review: seven added papers in Q1 journals and three in Q2 journals. This is not JCR or necessarily the publication-year quartile. Direct SCImago access was unavailable; accessible university catalogs and aggregators reporting SJR 2025 were used. The [quartile evidence table](../Relate_works.md#quartile-extension-20261030) gives the exact sources and category information. These rankings are editorial selection metadata and are not included in the scientific manuscript.
